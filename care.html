Caring for your Cocker Spaniel.


Everything you need to keep your Cocker healthy, happy, and looking their very best — from daily grooming to seasonal health checks.

Grooming . Nutrition . Exercise

✂

Grooming

The Cocker Spaniel's silky coat demands consistent, loving attention to stay in top condition.

Daily Brushing

Prevents mats and tangles in the fine feathers.

Ear Care

Essential for long, heavy ears to prevent infection.

Professional Trim

Every 6-8 weeks to maintain the breed standard look.

PRO TIP

Always brush your Cocker Spaniel before a bath. Water can tighten existing mats, making them nearly impossible to remove without cutting them out.

🍖

Diet & Nutrition

A balanced diet keeps your Cocker energetic, healthy, and at an ideal weight throughout every life stage.

Choosing a food

Look for a complete dog food with a named meat as the first ingredient. Avoid excessive fillers like maize or artificial additives.

Portion Control

Cockers love food and can become overweight if overfed. Use a measuring cup rather than guessing, and factor treats into the daily calorie total.

Feeding Schedule

Adult Cockers do best on two meals a day — morning and evening. Puppies under six months need three to four smaller meals to support steady growth.

PRO TIP

⚖️ Watch their weight

You should be able to feel — but not see — your Cocker's ribs. If in doubt about portion size, ask your vet to body-condition score your dog at their next check-up.
